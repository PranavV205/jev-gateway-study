# Arbor Pro Standing Desk: User Manual

Model AP-2 (dual motor). Manufactured by Halden Works Ltd, Portland, Oregon.

## 1. Specifications

| Specification | Value |
|---------------|-------|
| Height range | 62 to 127 cm (without desktop) |
| Maximum load | 120 kg, evenly distributed |
| Lifting speed | 38 mm per second |
| Noise level | Under 48 dB while moving |
| Memory presets | 4 |
| Power supply | 100 to 240 V AC, 50/60 Hz |
| Standby power | Under 0.5 W |
| Frame weight | 32 kg |

## 2. Safety

- Do not exceed the maximum load of 120 kg.
- Keep at least 10 cm of clearance above and below the desk while it moves.
- Do not let children operate the desk.
- **Duty cycle:** run the motors for no more than 2 minutes, then let them rest for 18 minutes. Continuous use can overheat the motors.
- Unplug the desk before cleaning or servicing it.

## 3. Assembly

You need a 5 mm hex key (included) and a Phillips screwdriver. Allow about 45 minutes. Two people are recommended.

1. Attach the two feet to the lifting columns with 8 M6 bolts.
2. Fit the crossbar between the columns and tighten the 4 side bolts.
3. Place the desktop face down and position the frame on it.
4. Screw the frame to the desktop with the 12 wood screws.
5. Mount the control panel under the front edge on the right or left side.
6. Connect the motor cables and the control panel cable to the control box.
7. Turn the desk upright with two people, then plug it in.
8. Run the reset procedure (Section 5) before first use.

## 4. Using the Controls

- Press and hold the up or down arrow to move the desk.
- To save a preset, move the desk to the height you want, press **M**, then press a number from 1 to 4 within 3 seconds.
- To use a preset, press its number once. The desk moves until it reaches the saved height.
- To lock the panel, hold **M** for 5 seconds until the display shows **LOC**. Repeat to unlock.

## 5. Reset Procedure

Run a reset after assembly, after a power cut, or when the display shows E5.

1. Press and hold the down arrow.
2. Keep holding after the desk reaches its lowest position.
3. After about 5 seconds the desk moves down slightly further and beeps.
4. Release the button. The display shows the current height when the reset is complete.

## 6. Error Codes

| Code | Meaning | What to do |
|------|---------|------------|
| E1 | Overload | Remove weight until the load is under 120 kg, then try again. |
| E2 | Collision detected | The desk hit an obstacle and reversed 2 cm. Clear the obstacle and try again. |
| E3 | Control box fault | Unplug the desk for 1 minute. If E3 returns, contact support. |
| E4 | Motor overheated | Stop using the desk and wait 18 minutes for the motors to cool. |
| E5 | Columns out of sync | Run the reset procedure (Section 5). |
| E6 | Cable disconnected | Check that both motor cables are firmly connected to the control box. |

## 7. Care

Wipe the desktop with a damp cloth and mild soap. Do not use abrasive cleaners. Check that all bolts are tight every 6 months.
