# Halden Works Ltd: Quarterly Report, Q2 2026

Period: April 1 to June 30, 2026. All figures in US dollars and unaudited.

## 1. Summary

Halden Works grew revenue 11.8% year over year to $4.82 million in Q2 2026, driven by strong business-to-business (B2B) orders. Gross margin fell to 38.4% from 40.1% a year earlier, mainly because steel and component costs rose. We shipped 9,850 desks, up from 8,900 in Q2 2025.

## 2. Financial Results

| Metric | Q2 2026 | Q2 2025 |
|--------|---------|---------|
| Revenue | $4.82M | $4.31M |
| Gross margin | 38.4% | 40.1% |
| Operating expenses | $1.21M | $1.05M |
| Net income | $0.41M | $0.44M |
| Desks shipped | 9,850 | 8,900 |
| Return rate | 3.2% | 2.7% |

Net income fell slightly despite higher revenue, because of lower gross margin and higher operating expenses. Operating expenses grew mostly from hiring: headcount rose to 142 from 128 at the end of Q1.

## 3. Revenue by Channel

| Channel | Q2 2026 | Share |
|---------|---------|-------|
| Direct to consumer (website) | $2.65M | 55% |
| B2B (office fit-outs) | $1.72M | 36% |
| Online marketplaces | $0.45M | 9% |

B2B revenue grew 31% year over year after we signed three new office fit-out clients. Marketplace revenue fell 8% as we reduced advertising spend on those platforms.

## 4. Risk Factors

**Steel price volatility.** Steel makes up about 40% of our component costs. A 10% rise in steel prices would reduce gross margin by roughly 2 percentage points if we could not pass it on to customers.

**Supplier concentration.** Northwind Supplies provides 64% of our frame and motor components. A disruption at Northwind would delay most of our production. We are qualifying a second supplier and expect to shift 20% of volume by Q1 2027.

**Currency exposure.** 18% of revenue comes from sales in euros, while almost all of our costs are in US dollars. A 5% fall in the euro against the dollar would reduce quarterly revenue by about $43,000.

## 5. Balance Sheet

At June 30, 2026, we held $3.1 million in cash and had $1.8 million of debt outstanding on a term loan due in 2029. Inventory was $2.2 million, up from $1.7 million at March 31, as we built stock ahead of the busy back-to-office season.

## 6. Outlook

For Q3 2026, we expect revenue of $5.0 million to $5.3 million and gross margin between 37% and 39%. We plan to launch the Arbor Pro in two new colors in September and open a showroom in Seattle in November.
