# Supply Agreement

This Supply Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 2026-02-16 between **Halden Works Ltd**, an Oregon company at 2100 Alder Street, Suite 400, Portland, OR 97205 ("Buyer"), and **Northwind Supplies Inc.**, a Washington corporation at 880 Harbor Road, Tacoma, WA 98402 ("Supplier").

## 1. Term

1.1 This Agreement takes effect on 2026-03-01 (the "Effective Date") and continues for two years, ending on 2028-02-29, unless terminated earlier under Section 9.

1.2 After the initial term, the Agreement renews automatically for successive one-year terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before the end of the current term.

## 2. Products

2.1 Supplier will manufacture and deliver the steel desk frames, lifting columns, and control panels listed in Schedule A (the "Products") according to the specifications in Schedule B.

2.2 Buyer will place orders by written purchase order. Supplier will confirm or reject each purchase order within 3 business days. A purchase order not rejected within that time is deemed accepted.

## 3. Pricing

3.1 Prices are listed in Schedule A and are fixed for the first 12 months after the Effective Date.

3.2 After the first 12 months, Supplier may increase prices once per contract year by giving Buyer at least 60 days' written notice. Any increase may not exceed 3% of the price in effect immediately before the increase.

3.3 Prices include packaging. Freight is charged at cost unless an order exceeds $2,000 before tax, in which case freight is free.

## 4. Minimum Commitment

4.1 Buyer commits to purchase at least $120,000 of Products in each contract year.

4.2 If Buyer's purchases in a contract year fall short of the minimum, Buyer will pay Supplier 10% of the shortfall within 30 days after the end of that contract year.

## 5. Delivery

5.1 Supplier will deliver each accepted order within 15 business days of acceptance, to Buyer's facility in Portland, Oregon.

5.2 If Supplier delivers late, Buyer receives a credit of 2% of the late order's value for each full week of delay, up to a maximum of 10% of that order's value.

5.3 Title and risk of loss pass to Buyer on delivery.

## 6. Payment

6.1 Supplier will invoice on shipment. Buyer will pay each undisputed invoice within 30 days of the invoice date.

6.2 Late payments bear interest at 1.5% per month, or the maximum rate allowed by law if lower.

6.3 Buyer may withhold payment of any amount it disputes in good faith, provided it notifies Supplier in writing within 15 days of the invoice date.

## 7. Quality and Warranty

7.1 Supplier warrants that the Products will be free from defects in materials and workmanship for 24 months from delivery.

7.2 Buyer may reject any delivery in which more than 2% of units fail inspection. Supplier will replace rejected units within 10 business days at its own cost, including freight.

## 8. Confidentiality

8.1 Each party will keep the other party's confidential information secret and use it only to perform this Agreement.

8.2 These obligations survive for 3 years after this Agreement ends.

## 9. Termination

9.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement if the other party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within 30 days after receiving written notice.

9.2 Either party may terminate immediately if the other party becomes insolvent or files for bankruptcy.

9.3 Termination does not affect orders already accepted, which Supplier will complete and Buyer will pay for.

## 10. Liability

10.1 Neither party is liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages.

10.2 Each party's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the total amounts paid or payable by Buyer in the 12 months before the claim arose. This limit does not apply to breaches of Section 8.

## 11. General

11.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Oregon. Disputes will be resolved in the state or federal courts located in Multnomah County, Oregon.

11.2 Notices must be in writing and sent by email with a copy by courier to the addresses above.

11.3 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's written consent, except to a successor of its entire business.
