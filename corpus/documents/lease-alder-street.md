# Office Lease: 2100 Alder Street, Suite 400

This lease is between **Kestrel Property Group LLC** ("Landlord") and **Halden Works Ltd** ("Tenant"), signed on 2024-11-12.

## 1. Premises

Suite 400, 2100 Alder Street, Portland, OR 97205, with a rentable area of 6,200 square feet on the fourth floor (the "Premises").

## 2. Term

The lease starts on 2025-01-01 and ends on 2029-12-31 (5 years).

## 3. Rent

3.1 Base rent for the first lease year is $31.50 per square foot per year, which is $16,275.00 per month.

3.2 Base rent increases by 3% at the start of each lease year after the first.

3.3 Rent is due on the first day of each month. Rent received after the 5th of the month incurs a late fee of 5% of the overdue amount.

3.4 Tenant pays its share of building operating costs (17.2% of the building total), billed quarterly and reconciled each year.

## 4. Security Deposit

Tenant has paid a security deposit equal to two months of first-year base rent ($32,550.00). Landlord will return the deposit, less any amounts owed, within 30 days after the lease ends.

## 5. Use

Tenant may use the Premises for offices and a product showroom. Light assembly of furniture is permitted. Manufacturing and storage of hazardous materials are not.

## 6. Maintenance and Repairs

6.1 Landlord maintains the roof, structure, elevators, and the heating, ventilation, and air conditioning (HVAC) system.

6.2 Tenant maintains the interior of the Premises, including carpets, interior walls, and its own fixtures.

6.3 Landlord will respond to urgent repair requests within 24 hours and routine requests within 5 business days.

## 7. Parking

The lease includes 12 parking spaces in the building garage. Tenant may rent up to 8 additional spaces at $150 per space per month.

## 8. Alterations

Tenant may make non-structural alterations costing under $10,000 without Landlord's consent. Larger or structural alterations need Landlord's written consent.

## 9. Assignment and Subletting

Tenant may not assign the lease or sublet the Premises without Landlord's written consent, which Landlord will not unreasonably withhold.

## 10. Renewal Option

Tenant has one option to renew for a further 3 years. To exercise it, Tenant must give written notice at least 9 months before the lease ends. Rent for the renewal term will be the market rate at that time, but no less than the rent in the final lease year.

## 11. Early Termination

Tenant may end the lease early at any time after the 36th month by giving at least 6 months' written notice and paying a termination fee equal to 4 months of the base rent in effect when the notice is given.

## 12. Insurance

Tenant must carry general liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence and name Landlord as an additional insured.
