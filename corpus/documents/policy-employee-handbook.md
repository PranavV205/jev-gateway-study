# Halden Works Employee Handbook (excerpt)

Version 4.2, effective 2026-01-01. Applies to all full-time employees. Part-time employees receive benefits pro rata to their scheduled hours.

## 1. Probation

New employees serve a 90-day probation period. During probation, either side may end employment with one week's notice. Remote work and tuition support are not available until probation ends.

## 2. Paid Time Off

2.1 Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accruing at 1.67 days per month of service.

2.2 Up to 5 unused days may be carried into the next year. Carried-over days expire if not used by March 31.

2.3 Requests for leave of more than 5 consecutive working days must be submitted to your manager at least 2 weeks in advance. Shorter requests need 2 working days' notice.

2.4 The company observes 10 public holidays each year, published in December for the following year.

## 3. Sick Leave

3.1 Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave does not carry over.

3.2 Absences of more than 3 consecutive days require a doctor's note.

## 4. Parental Leave

4.1 A primary caregiver receives 16 weeks of paid parental leave. A secondary caregiver receives 6 weeks.

4.2 Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

4.3 Employees must have completed 6 months of service to be eligible.

## 5. Remote Work

5.1 After probation, employees in office-based roles may work remotely up to 2 days per week, with their manager's approval.

5.2 Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 Pacific Time. Remote employees must be reachable during core hours.

5.3 Production and warehouse roles are not eligible for remote work.

5.4 The company provides a one-time home office allowance of $400 after probation.

## 6. Expenses

6.1 Submit expense claims within 30 days of the expense, with receipts.

6.2 Meals while traveling are reimbursed up to $60 per day.

6.3 Any single expense over $500 needs your manager's approval before you incur it.

6.4 Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours.

## 7. Training

7.1 Each employee has a training budget of $1,500 per year.

7.2 Tuition support of up to $5,000 per year is available for degree programs related to your role, after probation. If you leave within 12 months of receiving tuition support, you must repay it in full.

## 8. Leaving the Company

8.1 Employees with less than 1 year of service must give 2 weeks' notice. Employees with 1 year or more must give 4 weeks' notice.

8.2 Unused annual leave is paid out on your final paycheck. Unused sick leave is not.

8.3 Company laptops and badges must be returned on your last day.
